## One fact from AppWorld's leaderboard that changes the cost story (read Nov 20, 00:10 ET)

https://appworld.dev/appworld/leaderboard now has open-weight models at the top: IBM CUGA with GLM-5.3-Flash
(93.5% task completion on Test-Normal, 92.8% on Test-Challenge, Sep 7) and AtlasHarness with GLM-5.3 (94.0%
and 89.5%, Oct 21), above LARA MAS with Claude Opus 4.7 (88.7% and 85.6%). GLM-5.3-Flash is served at
$0.15 per million input tokens, close to what a small hosted model costs ($0.10). If the strong model is
almost as cheap per token as the small one, renting may never lose on dollars, and the case for coaching or
compiling has to rest on cloud calls, data leaving the device, and time. The pilot should report all four.
